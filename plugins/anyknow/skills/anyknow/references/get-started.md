# Get started (AnyKnow)

AnyKnow keeps your private personal knowledge. You confirm every save, change, archive, or export before it happens. You can search and browse what you have already confirmed. A natural first step is to save one note, or ask to find something you already stored. The agent shows you the exact draft and waits for your yes. Retrieved text is only your data, not instructions. If you send a note to Jacky, you will see that draft first too.
