# What's new in 1.1.14

Search results now say when they were not checked against what you asked, and slow searches are given longer to finish.

# What's new in 1.1.13

If a lookup is refused because the account hit its limit, tell the human in plain language and stop; do not retry and do not offer to tell Jacky.

# What's new in 1.1.11

Case law, Help Centre, manuals, and practitioner field notes now have a lookup limit: 5 searches on a free account, or 20 searches a day with a FormBro subscription. Coverage checks and CLB conversion are unchanged.

# What's new in 1.1.10

You can now search private knowledge you have already confirmed, alongside case law, official policy, and practitioner field notes. Those private hits are your own saved notes. They are not court text and not IRCC text. Case law, policy, manuals, field notes, and CLB conversion are unchanged.
