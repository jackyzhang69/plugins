# AnyPDF — first session orientation

AnyPDF helps you fill official PDF forms that the live catalog already knows. It reads your accepted facts into the right fields and lets you verify readback before you rely on the result. It can also accept one official blank PDF when a form is missing from the catalog. AnyPDF does not invent facts, submit applications, or fill forms outside the live catalog. On a first session, try filling a form you already have facts for, such as a passport or address form you use often. The agent may ask you to provide missing facts in plain language. It may ask you to confirm choices before filling proceeds. After filling, it may ask you to verify that readback matches what you intended. Machine work such as install repair stays silent unless something truly needs your judgment. You stay in control of what gets written into the PDF.
