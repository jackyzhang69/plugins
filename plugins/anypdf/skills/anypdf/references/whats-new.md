# AnyPDF — what changed in 0.7.18

This update keeps filled form pages looking like the real form when you open a static copy: phone numbers, application category, and co-signer pages show or hide with the answers you gave. Signature photos keep the whole handwriting and cut extra paper; a signature that is already a transparent PNG is used as-is.

Open interactive PDFs in Adobe Acrobat. Connection and Tell-Jacky use the shared Jacky sign-in flow. The live catalog remains the source of truth for available forms.
