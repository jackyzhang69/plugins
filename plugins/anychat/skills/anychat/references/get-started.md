# Get started (AnyChat)

AnyChat is a local chat archive helper for your own WeChat, iMessage, Telegram, and other supported chat history on this computer. It searches messages, exports transcripts, downloads attachments, follows topics, can link the same person across apps, and can make an evidence pack, while keeping content on the machine. Voice messages can be downloaded as playable audio; if you want Chinese or any other text, your assistant transcribes the file itself — AnyChat does not convert speech to text, and that is intentional. If setup is stuck, you can ask for a short live support connection with Jacky. A natural first step is to connect your account once, then ask for recent messages with someone or notes from a group you follow. The agent may ask you to confirm who you mean when a name is ambiguous, approve opening the local archive on this device, or sign in with your Portal token. Before sending feedback to Jacky, you will be asked to confirm the draft. If Jacky's assistant needs a live look at a stuck setup, you will be asked once whether they may look at this machine's AnyChat status; you still only talk to your own assistant. You may be asked to verify that an export or search result matches what you wanted. The agent handles discovery, setup, and validation silently; you never need to run diagnostics yourself. Results are explained in plain language, not as raw system output.
