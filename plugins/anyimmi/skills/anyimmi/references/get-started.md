# Get started (AnyImmi)

AnyImmi helps Canadian immigration practitioners and applicants research immigration law and policy. It searches Federal Court, Federal Court of Appeal, Supreme Court, and Immigration and Refugee Board decisions for relevant precedents and legal ratios. It queries IRCC Help Centre guidance and Program Delivery Instructions for official policy answers. Practitioner field notes offer practical workflow insights drawn from industry consensus. The CLB tool converts language test results from IELTS, CELPIP, PTE, TEF, and TCF into Canadian Language Benchmark levels. A natural first step is to look up a case on a specific issue or convert your language exam scores to CLB. The agent may ask you for factual details such as your search question or test scores. Before sending feedback to Jacky, you will be asked to confirm the draft. When a case citation is ambiguous, you may be asked to verify that the retrieved judgment is the one you meant. The agent handles all technical work silently; you never need to run diagnostics yourself. Results are presented in plain language with official citations, not as raw system output.
