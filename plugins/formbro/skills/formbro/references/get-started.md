# Getting started with FormBro

FormBro helps Canadian immigration consultants keep case data in one place and turn it into finished IRCC forms.
Through this plugin you can find clients and cases, read and update their data, create new cases from files, and check that a case is complete.
You can also produce filled IMM PDFs and have a local browser fill the IRCC and Service Canada web forms for you.
It never presses the final submit button; the person always submits.
A good first request is to ask for a case by the client's name and see what FormBro knows about it.
You may be asked for a few facts that are missing from a file, to confirm a change before it is saved, or to look at the browser window while a web form is being filled.
You are never asked to run anything in a terminal.
Portal passwords stay in the FormBro account settings and are never read out or pasted into chat.
If you are not connected yet, the plugin will ask once for your account token and then reuse it across all Jacky plugins.
