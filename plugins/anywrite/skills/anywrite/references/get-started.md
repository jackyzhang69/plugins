# Get started (AnyWrite)

AnyWrite helps the assistant learn and reuse how you write one kind of document. Give it a few finished examples; it compares their structure, tone, wording and layout, then shows you the method before asking permission to save it. Your example documents and their facts are never uploaded; only the exact method you confirm is stored privately in your account. For each new document, provide today's materials; missing information is marked rather than guessed. You can review, revise, archive, export or delete saved methods. The free plan approves one computer; before moving to another, remove the old computer under Account, then Devices.

Learning your tone means more than calling it “professional” or “friendly”: the assistant compares how you state facts, explain, disagree and make requests, keeping your usual voice separate from changes that depend on the situation. It shows short invented examples with the same facts so you can judge whether the wording sounds like you. A few examples cannot establish every preference: gaps and conflicting patterns stay visible, and a new audience, language or kind of document may need a different method.
