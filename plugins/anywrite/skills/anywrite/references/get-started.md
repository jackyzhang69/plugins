# Get started (AnyWrite)

AnyWrite helps the assistant learn how you write one kind of document and reuse that writing method later. Point the assistant to a few finished documents you wrote, such as letters or submissions of the same kind. The assistant studies their structure, tone, wording and layout, then shows you the method in plain words before anything is saved. Nothing is kept until you say yes to that exact method. Your example documents and the facts inside them are never uploaded; only the confirmed method is stored privately in your account. When you need a new document, give the assistant today's materials and ask it to write in your saved style. It uses only the facts you provide and marks anything missing instead of guessing. You can review, revise, archive, export or delete a saved method at any time. On the free plan, one computer is approved for your account. If you move to a new computer, remove the old one in your account page first.
