# What's new in AnyDoc 0.3.19

When you pack by a saved model, AnyDoc now shows the whole model before you say yes: how many kinds of documents the model has, how many this batch will produce, and which ones are left out and why — including ones you already have material for. Holding a document back "for now" is kept as a deferral for this batch, never as "not needed". After a pack is checked and delivered, AnyDoc clears its working leftovers (page previews, OCR text, review images and old drafts) and keeps your originals, the finished pack, the final plan and your approval. Your case files and facts remain on this computer.
