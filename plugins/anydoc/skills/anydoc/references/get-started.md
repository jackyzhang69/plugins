# Get started (AnyDoc)

AnyDoc helps you pack and assemble IRCC submission document folders from a client directory on your computer. It inspects PDFs, photos, and Office files, then turns your approved choices into renamed copies and assembled outputs in a new snapshot folder. It does not guess which documents belong in the package or claim an application is ready to file. A natural first step is to point it at a client folder and ask it to inspect the files, then review the proposed packing list before anything is written. The agent may ask you for missing facts about who owns a document or what a scan shows. It may ask you to confirm the packing list, including names, page order, and what to keep or drop. After assembly, it may ask you to verify that the written package matches what you approved. Machine work such as install repair stays silent unless something truly needs your judgment. You stay in control of what goes into the final folder.
